Society for the Neurobiology of Language 2026

Four interactive demos accompanying our posters. Each runs entirely in your browser — model coefficients only, no patient records are included or transmitted.

Poster 1
Predicting lesion from behavior

Build a behavioral profile with the sliders and watch the multi-task ElasticNet predict where the lesion sits across 64 left-hemisphere regions.
Poster 2
How much behavioral detail buys spatial detail

Four levels of behavioral granularity, from WAB subscores to 175-item naming errors, each read as a lesion map.
Poster 3
One model, read both ways

A single joint PLS model run forwards and backwards: set the behavior and see the lesion, or place the lesion and see the behavior.
Poster 4
Reading aphasia severity out of a stroke note

Type or edit an admission note and watch a text classifier score the NIHSS language item, with the phrases that drove each decision highlighted.
Brain visualization by brainWhiz. Roger D. Newman-Norlund, Saeed Ahmadi, Kalil Warren and colleagues · University of South Carolina · newmannorlund.com
